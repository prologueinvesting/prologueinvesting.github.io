Prologue artwork

These four watercolor illustrations were made with the built-in ImageGen tool. The prompts below correspond to the WebP files in this folder.

reading-table.webp
Use case: illustration-story. An editorial spot watercolor for the right half of a literary publisher's home page. Landscape 3:2 format. A single generous, sculptural still life of three old, everyday clothbound books stacked on a desk, drawn at a low three-quarter angle. Bottom book faded pine green, middle book muted brick red turned slightly forward, and a beautiful big open book on top, its cream pages fanning loosely to the left and right, a narrow oxblood ribbon trailing over the front edge. To the right side of the stack, one little blue-grey ink jar holding a graceful, spare olive branch. Grounded together with a small pale watery shadow. Place the arrangement in the middle; it should fill about 88 percent of the image width and 82 percent of the height. Full objects visible. Entire surrounding ground is unpainted near-white warm watercolor paper with lots of light right up to the edges, no rectangular background wash, no room details or horizon, no frame. Authentic loose traditional travel-sketchbook watercolor and small, broken sepia pencil marks on fine paper, dappled translucent pigment pools, dry brush, uneven edges, visible page lines, gentle characterful irregularities. Strong shapes, a lovely subtle patina, saturated book spines, a few warm ochre notes. No type, lettering, labels, digital effects, outlines around everything or other objects. Not a photo or 3D render.

salt-orchard.webp
Use case: illustration-story. A full-bleed landscape watercolor plate for a literary book jacket, 3:2 horizontal. A windswept orchard on a quiet Atlantic headland. One small white cottage with a rust roof in the middle distance left of center, an old twisted tree grouping in the foreground, golden grassy fields running down to an inlet and long bands of misty blue sea on the right. Pale, slightly overcast sky. Wind moving through the grass. The color story is slate blue sea and sky, warm yellow ochre fields, muted olive, touches of rust. Confident traditional watercolor on cold-press paper, gestural wet-on-wet washes with pencil underdrawing, luminous ink bleeds, beautifully spare forms. Painterly and literary, not overworked, not a photo. Composition has depth and reads powerfully at thumbnail size. Paint out to ALL four edges, landscape image only, no frame, no margins, no book mockup, no objects outside the painting, absolutely no text or lettering.

night-post.webp
Use case: illustration-story. A full-bleed landscape watercolor plate for a mystery novel's book jacket, 3:2 horizontal. In a narrow old British city street at dusk a late-night post office glows with two bright amber windows. The red-brick corner shop anchors the right half; a dark street opening and unfussy roof silhouettes recede to the left. A small unmistakable red pillar postbox sits on the pavement in the foreground. Reflected ochre light on wet paving. Expressive darks, traditional book illustration, twilight indigo and plum, deep brick crimson walls, warm honey window glow. Loose traditional watercolor on cold-press paper, a few quick irregular ink and pencil lines, real translucent pigments, granular blooms, simple confident shapes. Readable at thumbnail size. Paint out to ALL edges, no white margin or frame. Landscape art only, no mockup, absolutely no lettering, no signs with text, no typography, no photorealism or digital airbrush.

glasshouse-ledger.webp
Use case: illustration-story. A full-bleed 3:2 landscape watercolor illustration for a historical novel jacket. Old botanical glasshouse in a walled English garden in late spring, seen from just outside, slightly oblique. Long roofline and graceful weathered iron ribs under pale green glass, a partly open glass door near the center, a few terracotta pots, vines and large fern fronds in the foreground. Soft light, cultivated garden, no people. Muted verdigris and bottle greens with honey ochre sun and small coral terracotta accents. A real traditional watercolor on cold-press paper: generous loose translucent washes, restrained scratchy pencil marks, granular blooms, no exact mechanical grids. Painterly, gentle, simple enough for a small book cover. Painting extends to all four edges; no white borders, book mockup, frame, lettering, watermark or words. Landscape art only.
